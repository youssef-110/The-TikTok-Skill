# Viral playbook for carousels and short video

These are starting hypotheses. The account's own log beats this file. When `perf_log.py report` disagrees with a rule here, follow the data and note it in `config/account.md`.

## How carousels spread

Photo posts are pushed by: swipe-through (people reaching the last slide), time on post, saves, shares, comments, and re-views. Likes matter least. So design for **swipe**, **save** and **send**.

## Cover slide (decides 80% of the outcome)

- One idea, 3-8 words, huge text, high contrast. Must read as a thumbnail.
- Use a pattern that creates a gap:
  - Number + payoff: "9 psychology tricks that make people like you"
  - Hidden truth: "Signs someone is secretly jealous of you"
  - Identity call-out: "If you overthink texts, read this"
  - Story opener: "She left me on read for 6 hours, then called ME dry"
  - Contrarian: "Being nice is why they don't respect you"
- Add a small sub-line that raises the stakes: "number 4 is the hardest to spot".
- Face or eyes in the art pull the eye. Avoid busy art behind the title.
- Test covers, not whole posts: reuse a strong body with a new cover.

## Middle slides

- One point per slide. Title 2-6 words, body 1-2 short lines.
- Keep a numbered counter (03 / 09) so people know the end is close.
- Put the strongest point at slide 2 or 3, not last. Hook, then deliver early.
- Open loops: "the last one is the one nobody talks about".
- Same art mood and colour across all slides. Mixed styles look like a repost.
- 7-12 slides for listicles. Story/chat formats can run long (20-35) if every slide moves the story.

## Last slide

Give one reason to act, and only one:
- Save: "Save this for your next date."
- Send: "Send this to the friend who needs it." (strongest for shares)
- Comment: "Which one did you notice first? 1-9" (easy-answer prompt)
- Follow: "Part 2 tomorrow" only if part 2 will exist.

## Caption

- First line repeats or extends the hook. People read it while swiping.
- One question or one CTA, not both.
- Hashtags: follow the account rule. Mix 1-2 broad niche tags, 2-3 specific ones. Specific beats huge.

## Music

Photo posts with trending sound get extra discovery. `tiktok_music_trending` lists licensed tracks; the user attaches one in the publish form. Pick mood over popularity: dark, slow tracks for dark-psychology content.

## Posting

- Post inside the audience's active window (see account config). Post 1-2 hours before the peak, not at it.
- Consistency beats volume. 1-3 posts a day, same hours, so you can compare.
- Never delete a weak post in the first 48 hours. Some carousels start slow.

## Video specifics

- Motion or text in the first 0.5 s. No logo intro.
- On-screen text for silent viewers, kept out of the bottom 20% and right 15% (TikTok UI).
- Cut every 1-3 s for talking-head; let visuals breathe for aesthetic edits.
- Loop: last frame leads back into the first for re-watches.

## What to test, one at a time

| Lever | Example test |
|---|---|
| Cover wording | number vs story opener on the same body |
| Cover image | face vs object |
| Slide count | 8 vs 12 on the same format |
| Ending CTA | save vs send |
| Posting hour | 1 h earlier for a week |
| Sound | with vs without trending sound |
